# Artist statement — why this hurts to watch

A neighborhood is a grid. At first it is all cheap studios (dark grey).
Artists move in because they can afford it. They make it interesting.

Galleries follow the interesting. Each white cell raises the rent next door —
that is the whole cellular automaton:

> `rent += rich_neighbours × pressure`

An artist is a colored dot with an income. The rule is brutal and simple:

> `if rent > income → displaced`

No malice. Just math. The color drains block by block until the painting
is technically “developed” and emotionally empty.

## How to read it

- **Grey → white**: studio → gallery conversion
- **Colored dots**: artists, sized by nothing, priced out by everything
- **Sparkline**: the evacuation curve. It always slopes down.
- **CSV**: every dot that left, with its rent, income and tick. Data as memorial.

## What to try

1. Run **SoHo / slow burn** — notice how long culture lasts when pressure is low.
2. Run **Hyper / flashover** — notice how fast “revitalization” empties a place.
3. Crank pressure mid-run. That is what a rezoning feels like.

This is not a model of a real city. It is a 40-line elegy for every studio
that became a lobby.
